## 🛠️ How to Add, Edit, and Delete Products & Prices

You have full control over your product catalog right from your browser.

### Opening the Management Panel:
1. Open your website.
2. Click **"⚙️ Manage Products & Prices"** (in top bar, products section, or footer).
3. Enter your Owner PIN (Default: **`1234`**).

### What You Can Do in the Owner Panel:

1. **➕ Add a New Product:**
   - Click **"➕ Add New Product"** at the top.
   - Enter Product Name, Botanical Name, Price (₹), Unit (per kg/quintal/gm), MOQ, Packaging, and Shelf Life.
   - **📸 Real Product Photo Upload (Optional):**
     - Click the upload box to upload a real photo from your computer or phone (JPG, PNG, WebP).
     - Images are automatically resized and compressed for ultra-fast loading without slowing down your site!
     - Or paste an image URL or image path if you have one online.
     - If you don't upload a photo, it will automatically use your chosen icon/emoji with a beautiful gradient card.
   - Pick an emoji icon (e.g. 🌿, 🌾, 🌸, 🌺, 🍃, 🍂, 🌰, 🍵, 🌱) and a card color theme.
   - Enter a short description and click **"💾 Save Product"**.
   - It appears on your live website immediately!

2. **✏️ Edit Any Product:**
   - Click **"✏️ Edit"** next to any product in the list.
   - Change photos, upload new ones, remove photos, or edit prices, packaging, and descriptions.
   - Click **"💾 Save Product"**.

3. **🗑️ Delete Any Product:**
   - Click the **"🗑️"** button next to the product you want to remove.
   - Confirm deletion, and the product is instantly removed from the catalog.

4. **⚡ Quick Price Update:**
   - You can also type new numbers directly into the price boxes in the list and click **"💾 Save Live Prices"**.

5. **⬇️ Updating Your Live GitHub Pages Website:**
   - Whenever you make changes (adding/editing/deleting products), click **"⬇️ Download JS"** in the panel footer.
   - This downloads your updated `products-data.js` file.
   - Go to your GitHub repository:
     [https://github.com/guptaharsh6429-stack/BAG-BALAJI-ENTERPRISES](https://github.com/guptaharsh6429-stack/BAG-BALAJI-ENTERPRISES)
   - Click into the `js` folder → Click `Add file` → `Upload files` → Drag the downloaded `products-data.js` → Click `Commit changes`.
   - Your live website at `https://guptaharsh6429-stack.github.io/BAG-BALAJI-ENTERPRISES/` will now have the updated catalog permanent for all visitors worldwide!
