Who We Are

Committed to Purity, Quality & Pan-India Reach

Bag Balaji Enterprises is a specialized supplier and trader of natural dried flowers, herbal seeds, and botanical products located in Vijaypur, District Sheopur, Madhya Pradesh, India.

We bridge the gap between conscientious local growers and forward-thinking manufacturers in the herbal tea, wellness formulations, ayurvedic, nutraceutical, cosmetics, and food processing industries.

Every batch of our botanicals undergoes strict hygiene control, gentle shade/sun drying, and multi-tier sorting to guarantee vibrant natural color, intact essential oils, and maximum botanical efficacy.

✓ Ethically Sourced from Growers in Madhya Pradesh & Across India
